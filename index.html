Research

Research interest in computer graphics, perceptual rendering, computer vision and machine learning. Currently primar focus in neural rendering and novel view synthesis. First author papers are highlighted.

NePO: Neural Point Octrees for Large-scale Novel View Synthesis
Noah Lewis, Darius Rückert, Marc Stamminger, Linus Franke
Preprint, 2025
project page / more coming soon

Neural point octrees (NePOs) improve radiance field rendering for large-scale datasets by organizing point clouds into octrees, enabling efficient Level of Detail selection and real-time rendering of complex scenes.
